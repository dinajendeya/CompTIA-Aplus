# 1.3 Mobile Device Accessories

## Stylus
- Digital stylus for advanced writing.
- Communicates directly with device: pressure sensitivity, programmable buttons.
- Must be compatible with tablet (e.g., Apple Pencil for iPad).

## Headsets
- Hands-free audio: earphones and microphone.
- **Wired**:
  - USB connections common on laptops.
  - 3.5 mm TRRS connector (analog audio jack).
  - iPhone may use Lightning port.
- **Wireless**:
  - Bluetooth headsets.

## Speakers
- Mobile audio, battery powered.
- Wireless connection via Bluetooth.
- Stereo sound in small package.

## Camera / Webcam
- Internal or external.
- Commonly external on desktops, internal on laptops/tablets/phones.
- Used for multimedia apps and video conferencing.
- Real-time video communication.

## Docking Station
- Use external keyboard and mouse.
- Extend laptop interfaces and add functionality.
- Desktop adapter cards.
- Avoid cable issues.

## Port Replicator
- Similar to docking station but simpler.
- Usually connects via USB.
- No expansion card options.

## Trackpad
- Replaces mouse; useful in tight spaces.
- External options: battery powered, Bluetooth.
- Supports drag, tap, multi-finger input for right-clicking, zooming, window control.
- Enable/disable to avoid accidental clicks.

## Drawing Pad
- Use active stylus with external digitizer.
- Provides very precise input.
