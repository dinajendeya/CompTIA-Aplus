# 1.2 Connecting Mobile Devices

## Overview
- Phones and tablets: many ways to connect.
- Wired and wireless connectivity for synchronization, backup, and identification.

## USB Connections
- **USB**: Universal Serial Bus, high-speed wired communication.
- **Micro-USB**: Smaller USB, common worldwide.
- **Mini-USB**: Older devices, slightly larger.
- **USB-C**:
  - 24-pin double-sided connector.
  - Used for hosts and devices.
  - Supports multiple signals: DisplayPort, HDMI, Thunderbolt.

## Lightning Connector
- Apple proprietary.
- 8-pin digital signals for iPhone/iPad.
- Advantages over Micro-USB:
  - Higher power output.
  - Reversible design.

## NFC (Near Field Communication)
- Short-distance wireless data transfer.
- Built into phones for payment systems and authentication.
- Short range with encryption support.

## Bluetooth
- High-speed short-distance communication (PAN).
- Connects smartphones, headsets, health monitors, smartwatches, speakers.

## Hotspot/Tethering
- Turn phone into Wi-Fi hotspot.
- Extend cellular data to other devices.
- Dependent on phone type/provider; may incur extra charges.
